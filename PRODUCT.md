# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS: a single self-contained `index.html` (user's choice).

## Users

Owners of small and mid-sized distributors in India (FMCG, pharma, electrical, hardware wholesalers) and the staff who handle their orders. They are non-technical business owners. Today their staff retype every customer order into Tally by hand.

## Product Purpose

Munshi takes customer orders that arrive on WhatsApp, email, phone calls and photos of handwritten lists, and turns them into clean, correct entries in Tally. Success for the distributor: orders entered faster, with fewer mistakes, without staff spending hours retyping.

## Positioning

Munshi works inside the distributor's existing habits. Customers keep ordering on WhatsApp and the like, the distributor keeps Tally, and nothing is replaced. It reads messy real-world orders, including handwritten lists, matches them to that distributor's own product list and customer price lists, and learns each shop's way of writing orders from reviewer corrections.

## Operating Context

- Orders arrive on WhatsApp, email, phone calls, and photos of handwritten order lists.
- Munshi reads each order, matches items to the product list and the customer's price list, checks stock, and prepares a draft entry for Tally.
- A person reviews every draft before it is entered. Unclear orders are flagged, and Munshi can message the customer to confirm.
- Every correction teaches Munshi that shop's way of writing orders (e.g. a customer's "blue pipe 2 inch" always means one specific item).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Tally: works with TallyPrime only (confirmed).
- Data: processed and stored on cloud servers in India (confirmed).
- Pilot: short pilots, usually two weeks, with a few distributors, measuring hours saved and entry accuracy. Pilot price is undecided; do not state "free" or a price.
- Undecided / placeholder: WhatsApp contact number, phone number, email, company legal name, city, founder name.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Munshi (the traditional clerk who writes up a trader's books).
- Primary action: start a pilot via WhatsApp.
- Page background must be light (user requirement).
- Selling copy is English with Hinglish accents; Hinglish should be reviewed by a native speaker.

## Evidence on Hand

None yet. The team is very early and running pilots. There are no customers, testimonials, metrics, or logos to show; do not fabricate any. Order examples on the page are illustrative and synthetic.

## Product Principles

1. Nothing new to learn: the distributor's WhatsApp and Tally stay exactly as they are.
2. A person approves every entry; nothing goes into the books unchecked.
3. Accuracy over speed claims: show the checking, flagging and learning, not hype.
4. Speak like the trade: plain language a non-technical owner reads in seconds.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Owners are often 40+ and read on mid-range Android phones, some more comfortable in Hindi than English. Keep text readable at arm's length and keep contrast at WCAG AA.
