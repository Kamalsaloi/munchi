# Surface: landing page (index.html)

Mode: persuade. Audience: distributor owners and order staff (non-technical, often on mid-range Android). Action: start a pilot via WhatsApp (fallback: phone). Proof: a synthetic, labelled example order; no customers, metrics or testimonials exist. Constraints: light background, single HTML file, Hinglish accents (native review needed), placeholders for number, email, company, founder, pilot price.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the voucher-entry screen order staff already know, filling itself in while the visitor watches. It refuses the SaaS hero with a floating product screenshot and a card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Cool light screen ground #f3f6f7, white panels, deep teal title bars #0f5566, a yellow highlight bar #ffd94d as the selection, near-black ink. Red #b8321f only where a person must act (flag, Approve, the CTA). Anek Latin + Anek Devanagari, Kalam for handwriting, a mono for figures and keys. Key-rail buttons with visible key caps.

STORY: The owner sees their own morning (a messy WhatsApp or parchi order), sees it become a correct draft, sees a person approve it, understands nothing changes, and messages on WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left: bilingual billing headline (Latin over Devanagari), lede, red WhatsApp CTA and a call link. Right, at equal weight: the live voucher screen with input tabs (WhatsApp, parchi photo, call), the Sales Order grid, and a key rail whose Approve works.

FORM: The Voucher Screen, candidate 6 of 7, seed key 600dd588. Raises: Devanagari billing (film poster), marked draft/ink line (darkroom), strict state vocabulary (plankton), red reserved for action (magazine).
